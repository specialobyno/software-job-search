# Interview Story Bank

Reusable STAR stories (Situation, Task, Action, Result) for behavioural and technical interviews. `/onboard-me` collects the first ones; `/interview` picks the right ones for each stage and adapts them, and never invents new facts. Anything marked **[fill in]** is a detail only you know: add it once, and every future interview prep can use it.

Facts must match `facts.md` (Sections 1 and 2) and `profile.json`. If you tell a story differently in an interview, update it here.
